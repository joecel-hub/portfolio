Cookie Policy

Last updated: 8 September 2026 · Affects: gio-portfolio

In short: this website does not use tracking cookies or analytics.

1. What is a cookie?

A cookie is a small text file stored on your device by a website. Cookies can be "first-party" (set by this site) or "third-party" (set by other domains).

2. Cookies we use

We do not set any first-party tracking, advertising or analytics cookies. We do not load third-party scripts that drop cookies — including no external font services (all fonts are self-hosted on our own domain).

3. Local storage and session data

  • Admin area only: the site admin panel uses the browser's localStorage to store a login session token. This is not a cookie, is limited to the admin panel, and is used solely to keep you signed in to manage content. It is never used to track public visitors.
  • Public visitors: no storage, no cookies, no profiling.

4. Third-party cookies

None. We use no advertising, analytics or social-tracking networks that would place third-party cookies on your device while you browse this site.

5. Do Not Track

Because we do not track visitors, "Do Not Track" (DNT) browser settings do not change our behavior — there is nothing to disable.

6. Managing browser storage

You can clear cookies and site data at any time through your browser's settings. Clearing these will not prevent the public site from functioning correctly; it only logs you out of the admin panel.

7. Changes and contact

If we ever introduce cookies or analytics in the future, this policy will be updated before doing so. Questions: joecelpergis@gmail.com. See also our Privacy Policy.
