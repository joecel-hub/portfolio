Privacy Policy

Last updated: 8 September 2026 · Affects: gio-portfolio

This Privacy Policy explains how Stryg.Bytes, operated as an individual studio by Joecel Jaygee Pergis ("we", "I", "us"), collects, uses, stores and protects personal information when you interact with this portfolio website.

1. Who controls your data

For the purposes of applicable data-protection law, the data controller is Joecel Jaygee Pergis, operating as Stryg.Bytes. You can contact me at: joecelpergis@gmail.com.

2. What personal data we collect

  • Contact form. If you use the contact form, your name, email address, subject and message are sent directly to my email via your own email client (a mailto: link). No copy is stored on this website's server.
  • Review form. If you submit a public review, we store your name, role/title, review text, star rating, a consent record and a timestamp. Reviews are held pending and published publicly only after admin approval.
  • Server logs. Our hosting provider (Render) automatically records standard technical data such as your IP address, browser type and pages requested to keep the service secure and functioning.
  • CV download. Downloading my résumé does not require you to provide any personal data.

3. Cookies and tracking

This website sets no tracking cookies, uses no analytics, and makes no third-party advertising calls. All fonts are self-hosted — no requests are sent to third-party font providers. The admin area uses browser storage (localStorage) for a login session token; this is not a cookie and is not used to track visitors on the public site. See our Cookie Policy for details.

4. Why we process your data (legal bases)

  • Consent — for publishing a review or testimonial publicly.
  • Legitimate interest — to respond to inquiries, operate and secure the site, and showcase portfolio work (which inherently relies on displaying projects, logos and approved testimonials).
  • Performance of a contract — if we enter into a client engagement with you.

5. Where your data is stored

The website and its database are hosted on Render's free infrastructure, which may process data in data centers including Singapore, the United States and the European Union. We rely on appropriate safeguards (including, where applicable, standard contractual clauses) for any such transfer.

6. Who we share data with

We do not sell personal data. We share data only with (a) our hosting provider (Render) for the purpose of operating the site, and (b) publicly published reviews/testimonials you have consented to. We do not otherwise disclose your data to third parties except where required by law.

7. How long we keep data

  • Reviews / testimonials: kept for as long as they remain relevant on the portfolio, or until you ask us to remove them.
  • Server logs: retained according to our hosting provider's standard retention period.

8. Your rights

Depending on your location and applicable law (including the EU GDPR and US CCPA/CPRA), you have rights to access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability, and to object to processing. To exercise any of these, email joecelpergis@gmail.com.

9. Children's privacy

This site is not directed at children under 13 (or the applicable age of consent in your jurisdiction), and we do not knowingly collect their personal data.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top reflects the latest revision. Material changes will be noted here.

11. Contact and complaints

For any privacy question, email joecelpergis@gmail.com. If you are in the EU/EEA you may also lodge a complaint with your local data-protection supervisory authority.
