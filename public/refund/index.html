Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last updated: 8 September 2026 · Affects: gio-portfolio

This policy describes refund and cancellation terms for services provided by Stryg.Bytes, an individual studio run by Joecel Jaygee Pergis ("we", "I", "us").

For this portfolio website: no fees are charged for using this site, downloading a résumé, submitting a review or contacting us. There is therefore nothing to refund for the site itself.

1. Services and this policy

This policy applies to paid services (e.g. website design/development or similar) engaged with Stryg.Bytes. Where a separate signed contract exists, its refund terms prevail over this page to the extent of any conflict.

2. Cancellation of work

  • Before work begins: if you cancel a project before any work has started, any advance payment is fully refundable.
  • During work: if you cancel after work has begun, you may be charged (or retain as credit) the proportional value of work completed up to the cancellation date, plus any non-refundable third-party costs already incurred (e.g. domain, hosting, licenses).

3. Unpaid or advance billing

Any advance/deposit collected is applied toward your project. If the project is not completed because of a breach by us, the unearned portion of any advance is refundable to you.

4. Completed deliverables

Once a project is completed and delivered, it is generally non-refundable, consistent with the work having been performed — unless otherwise agreed in writing or required by mandatory consumer law in your jurisdiction.

5. Third-party costs

Costs paid by us to third parties on your behalf (domain registration, hosting, premium templates/plugins, licenses) are non-refundable once purchased, as those providers have their own policies.

6. How to request a refund

Email joecelpergis@gmail.com with your name, the project reference, and the reason for the request. We aim to respond within 7 business days. Approved refunds are issued to the original payment method within a reasonable timeframe.

7. Disputes

This policy is governed by the laws of the United Arab Emirates (subject to any mandatory consumer protections in your jurisdiction). Nothing in this policy limits your statutory rights.
